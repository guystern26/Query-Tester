New Custom Command

| aiguy prompt="..."

AI inside your SPL pipeline

Pipe any search results through aiguy and get an instant AI analysis — added as a field on every row. One pipe. Zero setup. Works in alerts too.

See It In Action
index=main
| stats count by sourcetype
| aiguy prompt="which sourcetype has the most events and why?"
▼ Results ▼
sourcetypecountai_answer
access_combined24,891access_combined dominates with 24,891 events (68% of total) — this is your web traffic. syslog is a distant second at 7,320. The ratio is typical for a web-facing environment.
More Examples
1 Use a preset mode instead of writing a prompt
... | aiguy mode="anomaly"
2 Focus AI on a specific field
... | aiguy mode="health" field="cpu_pct"
3 Filter rows with field + value — AI only sees matching rows
... | stats count by status, host
| aiguy mode="alert" field="status" value="Error"
   ^^ AI receives only the rows where status=Error
      (3 of 12 rows) — the rest still pass through
4 Use the AI answer downstream in your pipeline
... | aiguy mode="alert" | where ai_answer LIKE "%yes%" | sendalert slack
Advanced — Chain AI Calls
Rename the first answer, then ask a follow-up based on it
... | aiguy prompt="summarize the traffic"
| rename ai_answer AS ai_summary
| aiguy prompt="based on ai_summary, what needs attention?"
   ^^ second AI sees the first answer as a data column
      results keep both: ai_summary + ai_answer

⚠️

Scheduled alerts must have a cron interval of 10 minutes or more. aiguy checks the alert's dispatch history and auto-skips the LLM call if it ran less than 10 minutes ago. Alerts with shorter intervals will see aiguy_source="rate-limited" on most runs.
